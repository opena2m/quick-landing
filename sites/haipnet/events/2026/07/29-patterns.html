纹样之间

马耳他马萨斯卡拉市与中国都江堰市青少年图案创意工作坊

Between Patterns: Marsaskala (Malta) & Dujiangyan (China) Youth Pattern Creativity Workshop

📅 2026年7月29日 (July 29, 2026)   |   📍 中国 · 都江堰 (Dujiangyan, China)

活动概况

About the Event

跨越千山万水，当地中海风情与中国四川的天府之韵相遇，将擦出怎样的创意火花？本次工作坊以“纹样”为语言，让两国传统文化在青少年的手中相遇、融合，为国际友好城市交流注入面向未来的青春力量。
Crossing mountains and seas, what creative sparks will fly when Mediterranean charm meets the spirit of Sichuan's Land of Abundance? Using "patterns" as a universal language, this workshop allows traditional cultures to meet and merge in the hands of youth, injecting future-oriented energy into international sister-city exchanges.

重磅嘉宾与导师

Special Guests & Facilitators

莅临嘉宾：金伯利·扎米特（Kimberly Zammit）女士 — 马耳他驻华大使馆副馆长。她将亲临都江堰，观摩中马传统纹样的融合创作，体验中马文化互鉴，并考察都江堰市的城市文化与国际交流成果。
Honored Guest: Ms. Kimberly Zammit — Deputy Head of Mission, Embassy of Malta in China. She will visit Dujiangyan to observe the creative fusion of traditional patterns, experience cultural mutual appreciation, and explore the city's international development.
工作坊导师：扬·皮罗塔（Yan Pirotta）先生 — 马耳他知名平面设计师、插画师。他将全程主持工作坊，引导两国青少年展开跨越国界的艺术对话。
Workshop Lead: Mr. Yan Pirotta — Renowned Maltese graphic designer and illustrator. He will facilitate the workshop, guiding the youth in a cross-border artistic dialogue.

文化融合：传统与现代的碰撞

Cultural Fusion: Tradition Meets Modernity

在导师的带领下，青少年们将进行一场独特的解构与重组实验，探索以下两国经典视觉元素的创新结合：
Under the guidance of our lead designer, participants will embark on a unique experiment of deconstruction and reorganization, exploring the innovative combination of classic visual elements from both countries:
🇲🇹 马耳他元素 (Maltese Elements)
传统花砖纹样 (Traditional Tile Patterns)
马耳他蕾丝 (Maltese Lace)
马耳他十字 (The Maltese Cross)
🇨🇳 中国元素 (Chinese Elements)
青花瓷纹样 (Blue-and-White Porcelain Motifs)
传统回纹 (Traditional Meander / Hui Patterns)
祥云图案 (Auspicious Clouds)

友城情谊与未来展望

Sister City Bond & Future Vision

自马萨斯卡拉市与都江堰市缔结友好城市以来，双方在文化、教育等领域持续深化合作。继今年4月续签《2026—2028年度友好城市合作备忘录》后，本次活动将进一步巩固双边友谊根基。
Since becoming sister cities, Marsaskala and Dujiangyan have continuously deepened cooperation in culture and education. Following the renewal of the "2026-2028 Sister City Cooperation Memorandum" this April, this event will further solidify the foundation of our friendship.
