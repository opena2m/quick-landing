One platform, three capabilities

HAIPNET is the manufacturer, the network operator, and the rights layer — so a design goes from file to finished product without leaving the system.

Printer manufacturing

We engineer and build the industrial print heads, transports, and curing systems that print reliably across soft and hard materials at production scale.

Distributed print network

Every machine is a node. Route a job to the nearest capable printer, balance load across sites, and monitor output quality from a single control plane.

IP & license management

Designs carry their rights with them. Track ownership, enforce per-print licensing, and account for every unit produced across the whole network.

Print-stacking technology

From a flat surface to a finished object.

Print-stacking deposits and fuses material layer over layer, turning the same printer family that decorates fabric and metal into a true 3D manufacturing system. One material science, one controller, four surfaces.

  • Layer-fused 3D Successive layers bond into solid, production-grade parts.
  • Multi-material Fabric, plastic, and metal handled by one printer family.
  • Precision control Closed-loop curing keeps every layer within tolerance.
  • Production scale Repeatable output from prototype to full run.

How the network works

From an uploaded design to an accounted-for, licensed product.

  1. 01

    Onboard the design

    Upload artwork or a 3D model; rights and licensing terms are attached at the source.

  2. 02

    Route to a printer

    The network selects a capable node by material, location, and capacity.

  3. 03

    Print & verify

    The machine produces the part while inline inspection confirms quality.

  4. 04

    Account & settle

    Every unit is counted, licensed, and reported back to the rights holder.
