Print-stacking technology

The same printer that decorates also builds.

FLEXOFAB print-stacking deposits material layer by layer and fuses each one, turning flat-surface printing into true additive manufacturing. From a logo on cotton to a finished 3D part - one platform.

    DepositMaterial is precisely placed across the surface.

    FuseEach layer bonds to the one beneath it.

    StackLayers build upward into three dimensions.

    FinishA solid, full-color object comes off the bed.
